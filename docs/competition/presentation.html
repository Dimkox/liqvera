The problem

A price is not the depth
behind an order.

Missing context

A headline price omits available liquidity and the effect of order size.

Fragile evidence

A screenshot does not carry the inputs needed to reproduce its calculations.

Unclear assumptions

Users need capture time, requested quantity, quality checks and explicit limits.

Keep the report and its evidence together.

How it works

Capture once.
Check independently.

Public snapshot → Exact calculation → JSON + evidence ZIP → Offline verification

Observe

Public Hyperliquid BTC perpetual order-book data, captured with source metadata.

Calculate

Filled quantity, notional, VWAP, worst price and price impact for a hypothetical sweep.

Reproduce

Validate bundle integrity and recompute results without contacting the service.

No exchange trading. No fees, funding or net P&L calculation. Integrity checks do not independently authenticate the exchange.

What to inspect today

A report you can take
away and check.

  1. Open the wallet-free historical preview at liqvera.site.
  2. Inspect its capture timestamp, order quantity and depth results.
  3. Download the preserved report and evidence ZIP.
  4. Run the verifier matching that artifact's version.

The historical preview is labelled with its original capture time. It is not a fresh capture. Published historical bytes and their verifier contract remain unchanged by the current namespace migration.
